Every claimed system is public straight away, at its PluralKit ID:

```
https://pkviewer.xyz/s/abcdef
```

Choosing an address gives it a friendlier link. The ID address keeps working afterwards, and always will.

```
https://pkviewer.xyz/s/your-system
https://pkviewer.xyz/s/your-system/a-member
```

### What an address can be

Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. At least three characters for a system, two for a member, up to thirty-two. No hyphen at the start or end, and never two hyphens in a row.

A system address cannot be five or six letters with no numbers or hyphens, because that is the shape of a PluralKit ID and would collide with somebody else's ID link. Adding a number or a hyphen is enough — `clove` is not available as a *system* address, but `clove1` and `cl-ove` are.

Member addresses do not have that restriction, because they live under their own system.

### Changing your address

When you change it, the old address is **held for you for seven days**. Nobody else can take it during that time, and you can take it back from the management page.

Links to the old address stop working as soon as you change it — it is not redirected, because a redirect would outlive your ownership of the name and eventually point at somebody else's page. Share the new address.

After seven days, the old address becomes available to anyone.

### Releasing your address

You can give an address up entirely. The page stays public and goes back to its PluralKit ID address. The released address is held for you for the same seven days before anyone else can claim it.

### If an address is taken

pkviewer will tell you an address is in use or is being held, and when a hold ends. It will not tell you who holds it.
