Doughmination Music is a free public player.

*Maintained by Clove faerself.*

The concept was to bring free, zero-ad music to the public!

### How do I add music?

At the moment, only Clove can add music. However, there is a work-in-progress login system that's publicly available for authenticated users to send requests.

### I really want a song added!

Join one of the social media accounts below and request it there. This is currently the only way I'll be sure to see it.

### Some songs take a while to load!

This is a known issue, and I'm actively trying to find a way to make loading easier.

Doughmination Music is currently running from a single laptop, so the initial connection can sometimes take a little while to wake up.

**Workaround:** Play "_Rwar x3_" by Idfk who first. It's only 57 seconds long, and playing it appears to warm up the connection. Once it starts playing, other songs generally load much faster.

I have no idea why this works, but it works.
